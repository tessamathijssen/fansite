Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, often shortened to Hogwarts,
was the British wizarding school located in the Scottish Highlands.
Founded in the 10th century, Hogwarts was regarded as one of the finest institutions of magic in the wizarding world.
Any magical children residing in Great Britain or Ireland
who had reached the age of eleven by the start of the incoming term were accepted as students.

They were registered by the Quill of Acceptance and Book of Admittance when they first displayed magical ability,
and their acceptance was confirmed by owl post at age eleven.
However, if the child in question was Muggle-born, a special messenger from the school visited the child and his or her family to inform them of their magical heritage and the existence of the wizarding world.
