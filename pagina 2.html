Harry Potter is a series of seven children's fantasy novels written by British author J. K. Rowling.
The novels chronicle the lives of a young wizard, Harry Potter, and his friends, Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger,
among others, all of whom are students at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

The main story arc concerns Harry's conflict with Lord Voldemort, a dark wizard
who intends to become immortal, overthrow the wizard governing body known as the Ministry of Magic,
and subjugate all wizards and non-magical people, known in-universe as Muggles.
